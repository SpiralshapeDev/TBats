Terraria Bats Mod
Discord [ https://discord.com/invite/A7wBSSFfAD ]
Source [ https://https://github.com/SpiralshapeDev/TBats ]

The Terraria Bats Mod adds a new weapon type, the Bat! Bats can be very useful in situations where you need to deal with loads of nearby enemies at once or to hit an enemy away from you! Use them wisely though, since they're not great in one on one encounters, only for groups!
